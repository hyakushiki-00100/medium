**1/**
Deshan stayed talking with Longtan long after dark. When he finally rose to leave and found the night pitch black outside, Longtan lit a candle and held it out to him.

As Deshan reached to take it, Longtan blew it out. 🧵👇

---

**2/**
Decades before he became one of Zen's roughest teachers, Deshan was a monk so thoroughly versed in the Diamond Sutra that he'd written his own commentary on it. He set out to argue the southern schools out of their talk of instant awakening, commentary on his back.

---

**3/**
On the road he stopped to buy a snack from an old woman, who turned his own sutra back on him: the mind of the past can't be grasped, the present can't be grasped, the future can't be grasped — so which mind did he intend to refresh with that snack?

Deshan had no answer.

---

**4/**
He'd spent years explaining that exact line to other people.

Still shaken, he arrived at Longtan's monastery and stayed late into the night talking anyway. When Longtan sent him off, Deshan noticed the darkness on his way out and said so.

---

**5/**
Longtan asked him what he'd seen. Deshan didn't try to describe it: "From now on, I won't doubt a single word from any old master under heaven."

The next morning he carried his own commentary out in front of the hall and burned it.

---

**6/**
"Exhaust every subtlety of the doctrine, and it's a single hair set loose in an endless sky. Exhaust every trick the world runs on, and it's one drop dropped into a bottomless ravine."

---

**7/**
The night is on record far earlier than the version most people know — within about a century of Deshan's death, complete with what he actually said. The famous telling most people quote is a much later anthology piece, gathered after generations of retelling.

---

**8/**
Even the source retelling it centuries later refuses a clean ending: "Hearing of a man is no match for meeting him. Meeting him is no match for hearing of him." A couplet arguing both directions at once — refusing to let the encounter count as an uncomplicated win.

---

**9/**
The gap the candle exposed shows up constantly at work, usually dressed as expertise. Someone can walk a room through a framework flawlessly without any of it having cost them anything to learn — identical to real understanding, until an actual dark hallway shows up.
