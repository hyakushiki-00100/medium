---

**1/**
Deshan Xuanjian was one of the roughest teachers of his generation. One day he walked toward the dining hall carrying his bowls, ahead of the bell.

A young monk stopped him with a single question. Deshan turned around and walked back to his room without saying a word. 🧵👇

---

**2/**
The monk, Xuefeng, had asked plainly: the signal for the meal hasn't gone out yet, where are you going with those? Deshan didn't argue, didn't explain himself, didn't wait to be asked twice. He just turned and left.

---

**3/**
Xuefeng mentioned it to a fellow student, Yantou. Yantou's response wasn't approval of Deshan's grace under correction. It was sharper: as great as Deshan is, he said, the old man still hasn't grasped "the last word."

---

**4/**
Word reached Deshan. He sent for Yantou and asked directly: don't you approve of this old monk? Yantou told him privately what he meant; the text never records what was actually said.

---

**5/**
The next day, Deshan's lecture to the assembly was unlike his usual teaching. Yantou walked to the front, clapped his hands, and laughed: "The old man has finally gotten hold of the last word. From now on, nobody in the world can do a thing with him."

---

**6/**
That would make a tidy story on its own. Wumen, writing the case down centuries later, wasn't willing to let it stay tidy: if that really was the last word, he wrote, then neither Yantou nor Deshan has even dreamed of it — the whole scene is nothing more than a shelf of puppets.

---

**7/**
The koan actually stages two tests. The first: can Deshan be told he's out of step, by a junior, and simply absorb it — no defending himself. He passes instantly, wordlessly.

The second test is harder, and Xuefeng isn't the one administering it.

---

**8/**
Not reacting badly to being corrected is table stakes. Whether the correction actually landed anywhere is a separate question, answered later, if at all — by what someone does with it, not by how gracefully they took it in the moment.

---

**9/**
At work: the healthiest-looking response to a real problem is no defensiveness, correction taken on the spot. That's necessary, and on its own it still proves nothing. The harder version happens later, mostly out of view.

---
